About Us

Dedicated to empowering citizens through reliable digital services.

Welcome to CSC Nabagram Tathya Mitra Kendra

CSC Nabagram Tathya Mitra Kendra is a premier digital service hub located in Nabagram, West Bengal. We bridge the gap between rural communities and vital digital services. As an authorized Common Services Centre (CSC), we ensure quick, transparent, and hassle-free access to government schemes, financial tools, and digital solutions.

Our Core Mission

  • Digital Inclusion Bringing essential online resources directly to your neighborhood.
  • Community Empowerment Simplifying complex bureaucratic procedures for every citizen.
  • Reliable Support Delivering accurate documentation and prompt services with complete data security.

Why Choose Our Center?

Experienced Staff

Courteous and knowledgeable operators to guide you through every application step.

Time-Saving Location

Centrally located in Nabagram to save you long trips to district offices.

Transparent Pricing

Affordable service charges strictly aligned with government-approved rates.

Comprehensive Services We Deliver

Aadhaar & Identity

  • Demographic updates (name, address, date of birth)
  • Mobile number and email linking
  • PVC Aadhaar card printing
  • New PAN card applications and corrections

Government Schemes

  • West Bengal state scheme applications (Lakshmir Bhandar, Krishak Bandhu)
  • Central government welfare portals (PM-Kisan, Shram Yogi Maandhan)
  • Caste, income, and residential certificates
  • Digital ration card corrections and linking

Banking & Financial

  • Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AePS)
  • Domestic money transfers across India
  • Utility bill payments (electricity, water, gas, mobile)
  • Mobile and DTH recharges

Education & Travel

  • Online school, college, and exam form fill-ups
  • Admit card printing and result checking
  • Train (IRCTC) and flight ticket bookings
